# Editing your site

You can change your prices, photos and workshops yourself. Nothing you do goes live until you press **Publish**, and every change is saved, so anything can be undone.

## Before you start

1. Go to **app.pagescms.org** and sign in with the email address we invited.
2. Open your site from this link: **https://app.pagescms.org/<owner>/<repo>/edits** (bookmark it).
3. At the top, check the branch says **edits**. If it says anything else, click it and choose **edits**. Always edit on **edits**.

## Change a price

1. Click **Prices** on the left.
2. Find the service and type the new price. Numbers only, no £ sign (for example `70`).
3. Click **Save**.

## Add a photo

1. Click **Photo gallery**, then the **Add** button at the bottom of the list.
2. Under **Photo**, upload the picture (JPG, PNG or WebP, ideally under 1 MB).
3. Fill in **Description for screen readers**: say what's in the photo, like "A bike leaning on a stone wall above green fields". You can't save without it.
4. Add a short **Caption** if you like.
5. Drag the photo up or down to change the order, then click **Save**.

To remove a photo, use the remove button next to it, then **Save**.

## Add a workshop

1. Click **Workshops**, then the **Add** button at the bottom of the list.
2. Fill in the title, pick the date, and add the time, price, places left and a short description.
   - No date yet? Leave it empty and the site says "Date to be confirmed".
   - Full? Set **Places left** to `0`.
   - Have a booking page? Paste the full link (starting with `https://`). Without one, people register their interest instead.
3. Click **Save**. Past workshops disappear from the site by themselves after the date.

## Check the preview

After saving, wait a couple of minutes, then open the preview:
**https://edits--<site>.netlify.app**

This is a private copy of your site with your changes. Check it on your phone too. Not right? Go back, fix it, save again.

## Publish

1. Go to **<domain>/publish** and enter your publish password.
2. You'll see how many publishes are left this month, for example "7 of 10 publishes left this month".
3. Click **Publish to the live site**. The live site updates in a couple of minutes.

Save as many times as you like; only publishing counts. So make all your changes first, check the preview, then publish once.

If you see **"Monthly publishes used. Contact Over a Brew."** or **"Ask Over a Brew to publish this one"**, just send us a message and we'll put it live for you. Nothing is lost.

## Undo a change

Every save is kept as a separate version. In Pages CMS, the history icon at the top of each page shows your recent saves. To go back to an earlier version, or if anything looks wrong on the live site, message Over a Brew and we'll put it back.
